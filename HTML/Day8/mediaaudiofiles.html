Space Exploration

Space exploration is the ongoing discovery and exploration of celestial structures in outer space by means of continuously evolving and growing space technology. It involves the use of telescopes, spacecraft, and other technologies to study the universe beyond Earth's atmosphere. Space exploration has led to numerous scientific discoveries, technological advancements, and a deeper understanding of our place in the cosmos.

Space station

A space station is a spacecraft that orbits Earth and serves as a base for astronauts to live and work in space.
