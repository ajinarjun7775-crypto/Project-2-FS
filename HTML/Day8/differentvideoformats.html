Multi-Format Video Player

Demonstration of <video> element with multiple <source> tags

Video with Multiple Formats

This video player provides multiple format options. The browser will automatically select the first supported format.

Supported Formats in Order of Preference:

  1. MP4 (video/mp4) — Highest compatibility across browsers and devices
  2. WebM (video/webm) — Excellent quality and open standard
  3. OGV (video/ogg) — Older open format using Theora codec

How Multiple <source> Tags Work

The browser checks each <source> tag from top to bottom and plays the first compatible one. This approach ensures maximum cross-browser and cross-device support.

<video controls>
    <source src="video.mp4" type="video/mp4">
    <source src="video.webm" type="video/webm">
    <source src="video.ogv" type="video/ogg">
    Your browser does not support the video element.
</video>
