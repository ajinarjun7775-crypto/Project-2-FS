Water Treatment Plant

Chennai's Koyambedu drainage plant is a crucial infrastructure project aimed at treating and managing the city's wastewater. The plant is designed to handle a significant volume of sewage and stormwater, helping to improve the overall sanitation and environmental conditions in Chennai. It plays a vital role in ensuring that the treated water can be safely discharged into the environment or reused for various purposes, contributing to the city's sustainable water management efforts.

Benefits of Koyambedu Drainage Plant

  • Improved Sanitation: The plant helps in treating wastewater, reducing pollution and improving overall sanitation in the city.
  • Environmental Protection: By treating sewage and stormwater, the plant helps protect local water bodies and ecosystems from contamination.
  • Water Reuse: Treated water can be reused for various purposes, such as irrigation and industrial processes, promoting sustainable water management.
  • Flood Control: The plant can help manage stormwater runoff, reducing the risk of flooding during heavy rains.
