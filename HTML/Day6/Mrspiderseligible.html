About Mr. Spiders:

Mr. Spiders is a fictional character who has been rumored to be the next candidate for the mantle of Spiderman. He is said to have been bitten by a radioactive spider and possesses extraordinary abilities similar to those of Peter Parker, the original Spiderman.
